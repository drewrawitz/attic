# Attic

A personal, self-hosted record of the places its user lives and everything in them: what was done to each place, what they own, who they dealt with, and the paperwork that proves it.

## Language

### Who it is for

**User**:
The one person an Attic deployment belongs to.
_Avoid_: Owner (it reads as owning the Property, and a renter is still the User), household, account

### Places

**Property**:
A place the User lives or has lived, owned or rented.
_Avoid_: Home, house, residence. Never use "property" for the things inside a place.

**Current property**:
A Property that is still the User's today: it has no end date, or its end date is in the future.
_Avoid_: Active property, primary home

**Space**:
A room or area of a Property, such as the kitchen, the garage, or the exterior. Its size and what is on its surfaces (paint, flooring, counters) are facts about the Space, not Items.
_Avoid_: Room, location, zone, area

### Things

**Item**:
One thing in or on a Property, sized as one line on an insurance claim. A matching set (six dining chairs, a socket set) is one Item.
_Avoid_: Asset, possession, product, thing

**Fixture**:
An Item that stays with the Property when the User leaves it, such as a water heater or the landlord's fridge.
_Avoid_: Property item, house item

**Belonging**:
An Item that is the User's own and moves with them, such as a couch, a bike, or a car.
_Avoid_: Personal item, personal property, stuff

**Gone Item**:
An Item the User no longer has whose exit left a money trail: sold, given away, lost, stolen, or destroyed. An Item that was returned or thrown away is deleted instead.
_Avoid_: Retired, archived, discarded, inactive

**Price**:
What the User paid for an Item. Attic does not record appraisals, resale estimates, or replacement cost, so "what is it worth" means "what was paid".
_Avoid_: Value, worth, appraisal

**Part**:
A replaceable piece of an Item that is reordered by part number, such as a fridge water filter.
_Avoid_: Consumable, supply, accessory

**Category**:
A named group of Items from one shared, nestable list. Every Item has exactly one primary Category and may have extra ones.
_Avoid_: Tag, type, label

### Work and people

**Work**:
Something already done to a Property or to Items: a repair, maintenance, an improvement, or an inspection. Work is always finished; anything planned or in progress belongs to a Project.
_Avoid_: Job, task, service, ticket

**Cost**:
Everything paid for a piece of Work, materials included. Cost and Price answer different questions and are never added together.
_Avoid_: Price (for Work), total, spend

**Project**:
A folder for an effort on a Property that is still being planned or that takes more than one piece of Work, such as a kitchen remodel. It gathers Quotes, Notes, and Documents, and stays as the story behind the Work once it is done or dropped.
_Avoid_: Job, renovation, plan, to-do

**Quote**:
What one Vendor said a Project would cost. The paper or PDF it arrived on is a Document attached to the Project.
_Avoid_: Estimate, bid, proposal

**Vendor**:
Any business or person the User pays, buys from, or gets a Quote from: a contractor, a store, a landlord, an HOA, an insurer. Someone the User sells or gives things to is not a Vendor.
_Avoid_: Contractor, store, contact, supplier

**Schedule**:
A recurring maintenance task on a Property or an Item, due a fixed number of days after it was last done. A one-off reminder is not a Schedule.
_Avoid_: Reminder, task, to-do, recurrence

### Paperwork

**Document**:
A stored file about a record: a photo, a Receipt, a quote, a manual, or a warranty.
_Avoid_: File, attachment, upload, scan

**Receipt**:
A Document proving what was paid: a store receipt, an invoice, or an order confirmation.
_Avoid_: Invoice, bill, order confirmation, proof of purchase

**Note**:
A dated piece of free text for a fact that fits nowhere else, standing alone or attached to a record.
_Avoid_: Comment, memo, log

### History

**Change**:
A record of one write made through a tool: what it touched, as it was before and after. A Change can be undone.
_Avoid_: Revision, version, audit entry, history entry

### Dates

**Partial date**:
A date known only to the year or the month. It stands for the whole period it names.
_Avoid_: Fuzzy date, approximate date
